      How it works
      
      
      
        
      1 · Resolve
      Each favourite becomes a Qloo entity (artist, film, book, show…) with Qloo search.
      
        
      2 · Taste affinity
      For every person, Qloo Insights ranks places in your city by affinity to their own tastes.
      
        
      3 · Fair group fit
      Every candidate gets a score for every person. We rank by the least-happy person first, then the average, within your budget.
      
        
      4 · Explain & check
      We show the taste tags the group shares, and fact-check what a taste-blind LLM would have suggested.
      
      
      
      
      Affinities are aggregate patterns from Qloo, not facts about a person. TastePay sends Qloo only the favourites you type — never names. Payments run in PayPal's sandbox.
